# GeoInsight — Feature Ideas

## Data Layers & Overlays

### Environmental / Weather
- **Real-time weather overlays** — temperature, wind, precipitation, cloud cover (e.g., OpenWeatherMap API)
- **Air quality index (AQI)** — pollution heatmaps using APIs like AQICN or OpenAQ
- **Natural disaster zones** — earthquake, flood, wildfire, and hurricane tracking (USGS, NASA EONET)
- **Climate data** — historical temperature trends, sea level rise projections

### Demographics & Population
- **Population density heatmaps** — using census or WorldPop data
- **Urban vs rural classification**
- **GDP per capita / economic indicators** by region

### Infrastructure
- **Internet connectivity / broadband coverage**
- **Transportation networks** — airports, rail lines, shipping routes
- **Power grid / energy infrastructure**
- **Cell tower coverage maps**

### Geopolitical
- **Time zones overlay**
- **Country borders with disputed territories highlighted**
- **International shipping lanes and exclusive economic zones (EEZ)**
- **UNESCO World Heritage Sites**

### Terrain & Geography
- **Elevation/topography contour lines**
- **Tectonic plate boundaries**
- **Ocean depth (bathymetry)**
- **Vegetation / land cover classification (NDVI)**

## Interactive Features
- **Geocoding search** — search for addresses/places and fly to them
- **Distance/area measurement tool**
- **User-placed markers/annotations** with notes
- **Coordinate display on hover** (lat/lng/elevation)
- **Day/night terminator line** showing real-time sunlight
- **3D terrain/buildings** (e.g., CesiumJS, Mapbox GL)

## Info Panels
- **Click-on-country details** — flag, capital, population, area, languages, currency
- **Real-time ISS tracker** overlay
- **Flight tracker** — live air traffic
- **Ship tracker** — AIS marine traffic
